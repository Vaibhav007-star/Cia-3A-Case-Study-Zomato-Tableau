SLIDE 02: BUSINESS PROBLEM & CHOICE OVERLOAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Header: 02 · BUSINESS PROBLEM & MARKETPLACE DISTORTIONS
Subtitle: The Paradox of Choice in Food Search: Why One-Dimensional Sorting Fails Diners and Merchants

1. The Behavioral Dilemma (Paradox of Choice):
• Barry Schwartz (2004) & Sheena Iyengar (2000): In high-density markets like Delhi-NCR, consumers face 7,947 restaurants. Classical economics assumes more choice increases welfare; in reality, excessive choice causes decision paralysis, severe cognitive fatigue, and app session abandonment.
• The Two-Sided Platform Dilemma:
  - Diners: Default to simple sorting (popularity or rating) to reduce cognitive strain, missing truly relevant food.
  - Merchants: Emerging quality restaurants cannot compete with legacy chains' historical review volume, leading to high merchant churn.

2. Three Severe Market Failure Modes of 1D Sorting:
• The Popularity Trap (Sort by Total Votes):
  - Review votes follow an extreme power law: median is only 19 reviews, while top 1% have up to 7,931 reviews.
  - Sorting by votes promotes expensive legacy chains (mean cost ₹1,470 for two vs. ₹450 median).
  - Critical Failure: 70% of the top 10 most-voted restaurants DO NOT offer online home delivery!
• The 5-Star Fallacy (Sort Strictly by Rating):
  - Elevates low-sample boutique venues or unrated anomalies (2,139 unrated venues at 0.0★).
  - Ignores consumer budget constraints completely.
• The Hidden Gem Invisibility:
  - 194 elite restaurants achieve rating >= 4.0 with 53.6% delivery readiness at half the cost (₹800 vs ₹1,400).
  - Because votes < 200, they are pushed beyond page 10 of platform search results!

3. Three Core Project Objectives:
• Objective 1: Profile 7,947 listings and identify market failure points via automated ETL.
• Objective 2: Design an enterprise Kimball Star Schema for sub-second OLAP analysis.
• Objective 3: Build a Tableau Public decision-support simulator balancing Quality, Social Proof, Budget, and Service.

Speaker Notes:
"In Slide 2, we expose the business crisis. Default sorting creates the Popularity Trap: sorting by review volume surfaces expensive legacy chains averaging ₹1,470, and 70% of them cannot deliver food home. Meanwhile, 194 hidden gems with high ratings and 53.6% delivery readiness remain invisible on page 10. This drives consumer drop-off and hurts new merchant retention."

SLIDE 04: ETL PIPELINE & DATA QUALITY REMEDIATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Header: 04 · ETL PIPELINE & DATA QUALITY REMEDIATION
Subtitle: Automated Python Preprocessing (prepare_zomato_data.py): Six Verified Engineering Interventions

1. Missing Cuisine Imputation:
• Defect: 9 records had missing/null cuisine strings (0.09%).
• Action: Imputed as 'Not Specified' and parsed primary cuisine from 1,825 compound multi-label combinations.

2. Two-Stage Zero-Cost Imputation:
• Defect: 18 records globally (9 in India) had cost = 0.
• Action: Imputed via hierarchical median (City x Price Tier -> Country x Tier) to avoid division-by-zero in budget utility.

3. Cold-Start Rating Isolation:
• Defect: 2,139 listings in NCR (26.9%) had 0.0★ rating ('Not rated').
• Action: Created 'Is_Rated' flag and 'Rating_Status' to isolate cold-start venues without depressing active mean (3.28★ vs 2.67★).

4. Power-Law Review Scaling:
• Defect: Votes ranged from 0 to 7,931 with extreme positive skew (median = 19).
• Action: Applied natural log transform ln(1 + Votes) to compress extreme outliers and reflect diminishing marginal returns of reviews.

5. Spatial Coordinate Auditing:
• Defect: 497 records had invalid (0.0, 0.0) Null Island GPS coordinates.
• Action: Created 'Has_Valid_Coordinates' flag to prevent map distortion in Tableau dual-axis views.

6. Service Profile Synthesis:
• Defect: Operational services split across disconnected binary columns (Has Delivery, Has Booking).
• Action: Combined into a unified 4-tier 'Service_Profile' (Both, Delivery Only, Booking Only, Dine-in Only).

Speaker Notes:
"Slide 4 addresses the ETL rubric criterion. We highlight 6 concrete data quality engineering steps. We imputed missing cuisines and zero costs using hierarchical medians. Most importantly, we did NOT delete 2,139 unrated restaurants—we flagged them with an Is_Rated dimension to preserve cold-start onboarding while keeping our active rating mean unpolluted at 3.28 stars. We also log-transformed votes to handle the extreme power law."

SLIDE 05: ENTERPRISE DIMENSIONAL MODEL (STAR SCHEMA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Header: 05 · ENTERPRISE DIMENSIONAL MODEL (STAR SCHEMA)
Subtitle: Ralph Kimball Methodology: Separating Fact Measurements from Conformed Analytical Context

1. Architectural Overview:
• Visual: Full High-Resolution Diagram (Zomato_Star_Schema.png) embedded.
• Conforms to Ralph Kimball dimensional modeling standards for analytical data warehousing.

2. Central Fact Table (FACT_RESTAURANT_PERFORMANCE):
• Grain: Exactly 1 record per physical restaurant outlet profile per analytical snapshot.
• Surrogate Primary Key: Fact_Listing_Key (BIGINT).
• Additive Facts: Listing_Count (=1), Review Votes, Cleaned Average Cost.
• Semi-Additive & Normalized Facts: Aggregate_Rating (0-5), Log_Votes, 0-100 Relevance_Score.

3. Six Conformed Dimension Tables (Surrogate Integer PKs, 1:N Cardinality):
• DIM_RESTAURANT: Restaurant_Key (PK), Restaurant_ID (Natural Key), Name, Address, Chain Brand Flag, Outlet Count.
• DIM_LOCATION: Location_Key (PK), Locality, City, Country, Latitude, Longitude, Valid Coordinates Flag.
• DIM_CUISINE: Cuisine_Key (PK), Primary Cuisine, Category, Cuisine Count, Full Cuisine String.
• DIM_SERVICE_PROFILE: Service_Key (PK), Online Delivery, Table Booking, Active Dispatch, Service Profile Name.
• DIM_PRICE_TIER: Price_Tier_Key (PK), Price Range (1-4), Tier Name, Currency, Budget Classification.
• DIM_RESTAURANT_SEGMENT: Segment_Key (PK), LOD Segment, Rating Status, Rating Text, Strategic Action.

4. Multi-Dimensional OLAP Acceleration:
• Enables sub-second aggregation slicing, dicing, roll-ups, and drill-downs without expensive runtime SQL joins.

Speaker Notes:
"Slide 5 presents our Kimball Star Schema. In the center is FACT_RESTAURANT_PERFORMANCE with a grain of 1 row per physical outlet profile. It connects via surrogate keys to 6 conformed dimensions: Restaurant, Location, Cuisine, Service Profile, Price Tier, and Segment. This dimensional architecture gives us sub-second OLAP query performance and eliminates expensive runtime joins in Tableau Public."

SLIDE 06: MULTI-CRITERIA RELEVANCE UTILITY MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Header: 06 · MULTI-CRITERIA RELEVANCE UTILITY MODEL
Subtitle: Multi-Attribute Utility Theory (MAUT): Standardizing Dimensions onto a 0–100 Transparent Scale

1. Composite Scoring Formula (MAUT Framework):
Relevance Score = (wr·S_rating + we·S_eng + wa·S_budget + ws·S_serv) / (wr + we + wa + ws)

2. Component Utility Functions (0–100 Scale):
• Perceived Quality (S_rating):
  S_rating = (Aggregate_Rating / 5.0) * 100.0
  (Maps 3.0★ -> 60 pts, 4.0★ -> 80 pts, 4.9★ -> 98 pts)
• Social Proof / Engagement (S_eng):
  S_eng = [ln(1 + Votes) / ln(1 + 7931)] * 100.0
  (Log-scaled: 20 votes -> 33.9 pts, 100 -> 51.4 pts, 7,931 -> 100 pts)
• Continuous Piecewise Budget Fit (S_budget):
  - If Cost <= Budget: 100 pts
  - If Budget < Cost <= 2*Budget: 100 - 100 * [(Cost - Budget)/Budget] (Continuous linear penalty)
  - If Cost > 2*Budget: 0 pts
• Service Utility (S_serv):
  20 (Base dine-in) + 50 (if Online Delivery = 'Yes') + 30 (if Table Booking = 'Yes')

3. Four Pre-Calibrated User Persona Presets:
• BALANCED DISCOVERY (Default): Quality 40%, Popularity 25%, Budget 20%, Service 15% (General discovery).
• GOURMET / QUALITY-FIRST: Quality 60%, Popularity 25%, Budget 5%, Service 10% (Elite foodies).
• BUDGET HUNTER: Quality 25%, Popularity 15%, Budget 50%, Service 10% (Maximum value per Rupee).
• DELIVERY & CONVENIENCE FOCUS: Quality 25%, Popularity 15%, Budget 20%, Service 40% (Fast meal ordering).

Speaker Notes:
"Slide 6 explains our mathematical scoring model. All 4 dimensions are normalized to a 0 to 100 scale. Budget fit uses a continuous piecewise linear penalty function: 100 points within budget, smoothly penalized up to 2x budget, and 0 points beyond. Users can adjust weights using 4 pre-calibrated persona presets: Balanced, Gourmet, Budget Hunter, and Delivery Focus."
